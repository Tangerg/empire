# Built-in generation prompts

All references are the existing `assets/native/scenes/three-bridges.png`, except
wood cleanup which references its first generated transparent sprite.

## Ground — opaque

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: clean ground layer for an existing 2D tactical game, NOT a new map. Edit this exact 1672×941 three-bridge landscape. Preserve the original full framing, 45-degree illustrated perspective, color palette, contour rendering and every road, river bank, river channel, cliff, low rock and bridge-deck position precisely. Remove ONLY all four buildings including their cast shadows, all tall upright pine trees and the single upright round broadleaf tree, and the NEAR/FRONT railings and upright posts on each of the three bridges. Fill vacated areas plausibly with the immediately surrounding flat grass, dirt path or bridge deck. Keep bridge deck paving/planks, bridge arches/supports and FAR/BACK railings intact in their exact positions: they are fixed ground geometry, not standing foreground. Do not move, widen, shorten, rebuild or add any bridge, path, shore or river. Keep short shrubs/ground plants, cliffs and low boulders as ground details. No people, HUD, grid, text, new buildings, extra roads, decorative borders. This is a production clean floor plate; all removed upright objects will be supplied separately as transparent sprites. Do not recolor or redesign the recognizable existing scene. Match the input aspect ratio and pixel dimensions.

## Stone — transparent

Use case: background-extraction / game asset. Reference is the approved three-bridge tactical game. Create ONE independent reusable short STONE FRONT RAILING segment matching the front parapets of the two stone bridges exactly in drawn style, stone colors, line weight and 45-degree camera. Only a low row of grey stone parapet blocks with ONE square capped stone upright at the LEFT end, extending diagonally downward to the RIGHT at about 19 degrees. The right end is a plain clean vertical joint, no upright there, so identical segments can tile end-to-end. Three quarters view shows top coping and front wall thickness. Wide low object, roughly 3:1 silhouette aspect, a small upright rising above the low wall at left. Genuine transparent background, with absolutely no ground, river, grass, bridge deck, bridge arch, far railing, cast ground shadow, text or checkerboard. Complete object fully inside generous transparent margins, crisp hand-painted strategy game material, not a photo or 3D render. Preserve the reference's restrained saturated cartoon stone appearance; this asset is placed independently above an unchanged bridge deck.

## Wood — transparent, initial

Use case: game foreground asset on transparent background. Reference is the approved tactical map. Make ONE short reusable FRONT WOODEN BRIDGE RAILING segment, matching the lower-right wooden bridge's warm timber, bold clean illustrated outlines and 45-degree view exactly. ONE square vertical wooden post at the LEFT end, TWO horizontal wooden beams extending diagonally DOWN-RIGHT at 24 degrees to a clean right joint with NO second post, so copies tile end-to-end. Low simple bridge safety rail, no giant fence, no decoration. Show top and front wood faces, crisp readable grain, same hand-painted 2D strategy game look as reference. Real transparent alpha background, generous transparent margins. NO ground, grass, water, deck boards, stone piers, bridge arch, back railing, floating ground shadow, characters, text or checkerboard. Complete isolated rail only. Shape must be long and low, the left post rising just above the two beams; right end lower on the image than the left.

## Wood — transparent, selected cleanup

Precise cleanup edit for a game sprite. Keep the wooden rail's exact shape, two beams, left post, perspective, position, dimensions, colors and crisp drawing unchanged. REMOVE ALL brown haze, glow, halo, drop shadow and colored background around the timber and in the gap between the beams. Every pixel outside the actual wooden silhouette must be fully transparent alpha zero, with only a narrow normal antialiased edge. The opaque wooden material itself must have fully opaque alpha. No lighting bloom. Do not add ground or change the object. Return the same isolated rail on genuine transparency, not a black background or checkerboard.

## Broadleaf — transparent

Use case: background-extraction. Asset type: independent transparent standing tree sprite for this exact 2D tactical game. Recreate ONLY the small round broadleaf tree on the center-bottom riverbank of the reference (not a pine). Dense irregular rounded bright yellow-green/olive leaf clusters with darker green underleaves, a visible forked warm brown trunk, bold restrained dark outlines and hand-painted colored facets, viewed at the reference's 45-degree tactical camera. Preserve the original tree's compact short broad silhouette and modest trunk; no oversized fantasy tree. One complete tree centered with ample transparent margin, no cropping. Genuine transparent alpha background; absolutely no ground, grass, rocks, water, contact-shadow disk, glow, halo, backdrop, text, grid or checkerboard. Match the reference art style, light from upper left. It will be a small independently depth-sorted sprite, not a whole environment painting.
