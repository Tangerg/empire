# c02-status-local-support

## Verified identity
Four armored forearms converge into one central glove-stack/support gesture: blue upper left, olive upper right, blue with pale cuff lower left, amber with pale cuff lower right. One solid cyan ring remains behind the hands.

## Native 32px review
At actual 32px the four-arm X silhouette, contrasting sleeve colors, shared central clasp and cyan ring read on white/black/olive. Individual finger anatomy is not a native32 claim.

## Reconstruction caveats
The original board cuts the four sleeve ends at its crop edges. The remake reconstructs minimal closed sleeve ends inside the new canvas, without inventing additional arms. The source glow is replaced by a bounded solid cyan ring. The original overlapping hand stack is abstracted as broad glove segments.

## Alpha and provenance
Raw and master bytes match. Source canvas edge max alpha: 1. Two-pixel native border max alpha: 0. Full native white, source512 black/white/olive and native32 black/white/olive were visually reviewed. No source pixel cleanup or recolor.

## Scope
Source ready for parent integration only. C02 catalog-only; no authored consumer, formal runtime count or live HUD claim. 32x32, anchor16,16, no HD.
