Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: opaque full-bleed square GRASS MATERIAL for a hand-painted graphic strategy game terrain renderer.
Primary request: Create only a continuous flat top-down grass surface filling every pixel of a square image, designed to be clipped by existing ground masks. Match a bold illustrated medieval strategy-game land treatment: broad connected matte olive-green and muted gray-green color planes, irregular angular broken boundary accents, sparse small GROUPS of simple grass marks. Large quiet areas dominate. Shape edges are crisp with an organic hand-cut zigzag quality. Only about 6 to 10 broad interlocking patches across the entire square, plus a few smaller edge fragments. Subtle flat shading, simplified painted blocks, not photographic texture.
Color palette: muted olive mid-green, gray forest green, restrained yellow-olive highlights. No neon green.
Composition: uniform top-down material surface, edge-to-edge coverage, no perspective or horizon, no focal point, no vignette. A material swatch, not a whole scene background.
Constraints: genuinely opaque, square, full-bleed; no objects, trees, bushes, characters, paths, roads, banks, water, rocks, borders, text or symbols. No fine stippling, micro-noise, grain, tiny repeated leaves, isolated confetti shapes, gradients or 3D lighting. No individual large grass clumps that read as placed props. Favor large connected graphic planes and very sparse grouped marks.
