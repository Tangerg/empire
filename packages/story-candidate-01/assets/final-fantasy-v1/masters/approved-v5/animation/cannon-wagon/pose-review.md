# Cannon wagon quartet review

Catalog-only canonical art. This is not the rules-derived GrayFlag supply wagon and does not replace it on maps.

- Idle: crew and wheels at rest, unfired brass cannon
- Walk-a: forward extended boot, opposite trailing boot lifted, wheel spokes rotated
- Attack: grounded bracing, barrel recoil, fluttered orange panel, short muzzle flash and small smoke puff
- Walk-b: planted left-side boot and lifted right-side boot give a clear alternating step; wheel spokes have a distinct phase

All four native masters are1536×1024 RGBA with byte-identical raw copies. The one scale is88/1449, selected by the coordinator from the infantry/ballista comparison. Registration is wheel-contact-based, with translations no greater than1.215 logical pixels. The proposed full frame is112×72 with origin[8,4] and anchor[63.8730158730,61.2698412698]. All full native canvases fit without crop or independent resizing.

The accepted silhouettes have no alpha>1 on the native perimeter. Native alpha1 specks and alpha peak254 are preserved. Black/white composites and common-scale native reviews pass. The attack effect has63 source pixels of opaque right margin. No permanent exterior glow, background or floor is present.

The original unit atlas includes one crew member; adding that person is not an invention. The original tiny runtime strip accidentally includes unrelated troll-row fragments along its bottom; those fragments are not part of the canonical cannon subject and were not carried forward.
