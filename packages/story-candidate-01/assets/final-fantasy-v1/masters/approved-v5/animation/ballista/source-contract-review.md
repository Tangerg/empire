# Ballista source contract

C01-UNIT-BALLISTA binds both c01.ballista and built-in ballista. Original runtime strip is 256x48 with four 64x48 frames in idle, walk-a, attack, walk-b order, anchor [32,47], rules footprint [2,2]. HD strip is 512x96 with four 128x96 frames.

## Original identity
The first row of combat-atlas-ballista-cavalry-battlemage-eagle.png is the actual unit: one steel-helmeted imperial operator in navy/red armor, controlling a heavy wooden four-wheel crossbow cart. Keep the operator, thick structural wood, metal corner brackets, wheel hubs, rope string and right-facing steel-tipped bolt. The separate ballista deployment structure and equipment topic do not replace this combat unit.

## Action semantics
c01.ballista is a heavy siege/ranged unit with attack and wait. Its weapon fires piercing bolts at range 2-4, cannot counter and cannot move-and-attack. The four-frame contract remains idle, walk-a, attack, walk-b. Future walk frames should show opposite operator steps plus wheel/lever motion; attack should show an unmistakable release/recoil or cocking/shooting action with the same mechanism. No cannon barrel, fireball or invented crew. Idle must be reviewed before the other three frames.
