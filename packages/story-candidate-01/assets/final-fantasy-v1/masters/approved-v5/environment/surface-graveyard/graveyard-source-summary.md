## Review and sampling

A 256 × 256 logical world period was approved as the first sampling proposal, not a final scene binding. Accent groups are sparse and much reduced from the original material, but many main stones and tufts would read around 6–18 world units at that period, larger than the initial 2–6-unit guidance. Each source has recognizable broad matte soil patches. All retain fine low-contrast soil mottling and ragged micro edges, rather than the completely clean approved earth-plane interiors requested. Native visual review records this limitation; the 256-pixel diagnostic samples are quieter. No automatic further image calls or programmatic filtering were used to conceal it.

Native opposite-edge mean absolute RGB differences are approximately 4.1–6.2 out of 255. These are measurements, not seamlessness claims. Unblended 2 × 2 repeat diagnostics reveal remaining boundaries. Parent owns authorized seam treatment, world UV and actual c01-11/c01-16 scene review. All sources have visible colored soil midtones; color and low-luminance fractions are recorded in the aggregate QA.
