## What remains uncertain

The new high cohorts appear within one83⅓ms interval when their predecessors impact. Their entry is not progressively revealed in a preceding frame. The dominant upper drop therefore changes from left to center to right to left, and same-column nearest-form tracking would see a large upward reset. Static decoded frames make those substitutions visibly identifiable; they do not prove the substitutions will read as smooth rain rather than pops at12fps.

The falling steps are also uneven: for example, the center advances244,185,32source pixels over successive surviving frames. That may create a perceptible slow beat near4→1. The wide crown gives a clear impact beat, which may be appropriate for stylized rain but is not evidence of steady rainfall velocity.

Eight uninterrupted cycles have not been visually observed because browser launch/URL routes were blocked. The exact APNGs, native composites and labeled full/seam strips are provided, but smooth perceived playback and final semantic approval remain pending. No claim is made that distinct frame hashes establish motion or that every apparent drop is the same individual.
