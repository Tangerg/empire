# FX10 static cohort and registration review

## What is established

The source remains two gray-brown stones surrounded by broken blue-gray ellipses. The four drawings are independent built-in imagegen outputs, not duplicated, translated, faded, warped or synthesized copies. The same whole-canvas transform is applied to all four.

Both stones stay registered. Diagnostic warm-color contour bounds vary by at most 2 source pixels, equivalent to 0.045 logical pixels. Large-stone contact bottoms are 806, 807, 807, 808; small-stone contact bottoms are 820 in every phase. Their different fixed baseline heights reflect the original shallow-water perspective. They are visually stationary at native 32 and 64 pixels, but not byte-identical in contour or shading.

The labeled full 1–2–3–4 and seam 3–4–1–2 sheets were inspected, along with native 32/64 and zoomed composites on black, white, bright stone, green ground, dark earth and checker. The stones remain legible. Thin ripples are subtler on green/dark ground at 32 pixels. There is no backing rectangle, visible source-edge contamination or crop clipping.

- The near phase's middle left sweep progresses x289 → x213 → x148, then becomes far-side fragments at x89 in renewal. Those old fragments retire rather than contracting to the new inner ring
- A new smaller left cohort appears in growing at x411 and advances to x397 and x383; interpreting the next near middle sweep at x289 as its successor gives a much larger 94-pixel seam step. This is outward, but the cadence is uneven

The outer fragments disappear by cohort while the replacement inner arcs remain. No frame is empty, and the seam does not require an old outer ring to shrink back toward the stones.

## Explicit continuity caveats

Individual front-arc identity is less certain than the side arcs. Wide contains a center segment at y845–869, lower pieces at y881–930 and an oldest front segment at y944–969. Renewal contains new inner y822–833, another center segment y840–856, split front pieces y864–894 and outgoing low fragments y927–949.

Do not match the wide center segment directly to renewal's center segment and call that outward motion: that naive match would move inward/upward. The intended interpretation is overlapping generations, with older segments advancing or splitting and new inner arcs appearing. These four drawings do not establish a unique identity for every front fragment. The static sequence is plausible; it does not prove smooth perceived motion.

Total source alpha mass is 41115, 44828, 51254 and 41931. Wide-to-renewal drops 18.2%; renewal-to-near drops 1.9%. The replacement cohort repair removed the earlier missing-generation defect, but some density pulse remains possible. Renewal water is also slightly darker/bluer than near. Both variations are authored in the unmodified sources.

## Playback boundary

The 14 exact-speed APNGs have four equal 1/12-second frames, a 1/3-second cycle and infinite repeat. Another 14 APNGs are slowed diagnostics at 3 fps. All 112 decoded frames were checked against the expected exports/composites. This verifies encoding and timing, not observed motion.

Eight uninterrupted cycles were not observed in a browser. The parent reported a known blocked browser/GPU environment; no further escalation was attempted. No authored campaign consumer currently references this effect. This is catalog-ready artwork and offline QA, not live gameplay or deployment evidence.
