# c02-status-star-vein-energy

## Verified identity
One central elongated violet crystal, eight surrounding shards, magenta upper/middle branching energy veins and cyan lower branching vein are retained. The main warm pale central energy node remains visible.

## Native 32px review
At actual 32px the central gem, violet satellite group, magenta energy center and cyan lower section remain readable across white/black/olive. All eight satellites exist in the mother; the two smallest bottom shards reduce to tiny pixels, so individual shard counting at 32px is not a guaranteed user interaction.

## Reconstruction caveats
