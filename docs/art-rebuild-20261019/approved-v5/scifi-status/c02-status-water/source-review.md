# Source reconstruction

Source: Board 01, third visual row, leftmost complete canister.

Complete motif: One upright cyan reservoir canister, broad gray end collars, two side handles/rails, a large water-drop medallion, and amber cap.

Old runtime fragments: The runtime retains the cyan cylinder and dark oval but compresses the droplet into a tiny light spot; fine pipes and brackets collapse into gray-blue fragments. Detached bottom pixels have no independent source object and are treated as legacy extraction residue, not motif.

Uncertainty: No uncertainty about subject identity or the one-canister count. Tiny fastener count and small surface reflections are not recoverable at runtime and are intentionally simplified.

The full source board and exact runtime were inspected before generation. Mapping was visual, not manifest-slot sorting. Background gradients are source-board presentation and were not carried into the isolated sprite.
