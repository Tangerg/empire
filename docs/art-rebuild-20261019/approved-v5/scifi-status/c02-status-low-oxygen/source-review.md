Complete motif: A respirator helmet and cropped shoulders with cyan visor; foreground round oxygen gauge at lower left and red warning triangle at lower right. Three cyan bubbles at upper right and a subordinate olive leaf accent at the lower edge preserve supporting source details.

Old runtime fragments: The runtime retains a cyan helmet blob, lower cyan/red circle, red right-hand warning fragment, right-side cyan dots and green bottom speck. The warning mark and respirator filter are largely lost, while the gauge and helmet merge through fragmented armor.

Uncertainty: The low-oxygen identity, helmet, gauge and warning triangle are unambiguous. Tiny gauge graduations do not encode a documented numeric value; they were omitted. Source foliage leaf count is ambiguous at tiny size and was simplified to two broad leaves. Three major bubbles are preserved; one tiny decorative dot is omitted.

The full source board and exact runtime were inspected before generation. Mapping was visual, not manifest-slot sorting. Background gradients are source-board presentation and were not carried into the isolated sprite.
