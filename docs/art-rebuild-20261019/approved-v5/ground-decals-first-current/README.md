# Six approved small ground accents

Full actual c01-02 BoardView retains every actor, rule building, terrain cell and scenery placement. Six atlas identities were replaced with broad grass, stone and fallen-leaf groups; original caller scale and 0.72 opacity remain. The source6×4 grid and exact64/128 atlas contract are preserved, and all unselected cells remain byte-identical to their immutable baseline.

The two images compare existing ground-edge overlays, not a before/after of all art. Contact boards in environment-runtime-decals-first/second and their -before folders isolate the actual old/new decal pixels. Mud, roots, abandoned cart-stop piles, hill caps and remaining legacy units are still visibly old and are not accepted as complete. Offline SVG rasterization is not live browser or GPU playback.
