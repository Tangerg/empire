# Soler broad matte v2: bounded source corrections

2026-10-20. Exactly one built-in image_gen.imagegen edit per original source. All four original masters were inspected before editing, and all four complete output images were visually inspected. The explicit goal was eliminating excessive secondary surface mottling, not redesigning subjects or globally recoloring them. No CLI, code repaint, palette repair, crop, filter, or regenerated runtime source was used.

## Deliverables and preservation

Each existing canonical-terrain/soler-* directory has a new candidates/broad-matte-v2 directory containing raw.png, master.png, the exact prompt.txt and request.json, and provenance.json. Raw and master are identical copies of the corresponding generator PNG. Every candidate is opaque RGB, 1254 × 1254. Original master SHA-256 values were checked after edits and remain unchanged; see source-integrity.json. No original source/raw, catalog, shared exporter, runtime, manifest or Git operation was authored by this task.

proposed-registration.json is a proposal only. It retains original source size and exact full-square/quadrant sampling windows while pointing to the versioned candidates and their hashes. No candidate has been selected by this worker. full-source-comparison.html displays original and candidate at unscaled full source dimensions, side by side; it references their actual immutable PNGs rather than a repainted or resampled comparison image.

## Full-source visual judgment and geometry drift

These are genuine substantial full-source surface simplifications. Acceptance is not based on downsample hiding defects. However, an image-generation edit is not a pixel-locked recoloring operation: zero contour drift cannot be claimed. The native alpha geometry is independently byte-identical, as discussed below.

- Blue ice: the dense mottled patches across every pale top are removed. Broad pale-blue tops and cobalt crevices remain, and the four-quadrant atlas structure, large-slab placement, small intervening stones and crevice network are recognizably retained. Some individual outline vertices and side-face facets are smoothed or reinterpreted. For example, the central large slab in the top-left quadrant has a cleaner continuous top plane where internal highlight patches previously fragmented it; its lower side facets are coarser. In the lower-left quadrant, the upper-left slab's bottom edge and side-face subdivisions are simplified. There is no slab-network or quadrant rearrangement, but do not label all boundaries pixel-identical. Residual broad side facets and a faint smooth tonal rolloff remain; this is markedly flatter, not literally one exact RGB per face.
- Brittle ice: mottled cyan patches are replaced by broad pale-cyan planes. The three principal large plates down the center, dark branching crevices, and prominent white fractures remain in place. Fine fracture tips and small white rim irregularities are simplified or redrawn; in particular the large lower-center plate's thin secondary hairlines are not all retained identically. Existing plate topology remains readable, with no new plate network. This is the greatest geometry/detail-preservation caveat of the four. White edge lines are cleaner and locally more prominent; contrast is not exactly unchanged. Do not accept this as strict pixel-locked preservation of every hairline.
- Deep factory: diagonal mottled islands are removed across all large navy panels. Plate corners, seams, fastener arrangement and centered six-opening ventilation grille remain visually stable in the full image. The grille and its outer housing are not replaced with a different object. Tiny edge antialiasing and shading differences exist; exact pixel geometry is not asserted. Some very smooth tonal variation remains, but no secondary mottled field dominates the plates. This is the strongest bounded correction.
- Heated walkway: mottled diagonal islands are removed from the charcoal panels and central octagonal plate. The segmented north/east/south/west orange heating cross, unlit center, bolts and seam arrangement remain visually stable. Orange centers retain yellow cores; geometry and layout were not intentionally redesigned. Tiny bevel/antialias differences remain. The output is broad matte rather than a rigorously indexed flat-color image.

Recommendation: accept deep factory and heated walkway as bounded broad-matte corrections. Blue ice is also a strong stylistic improvement if minor generative contour drift is acceptable. Brittle ice is conditional because some hairline fracture detail was simplified; retain the original if the acceptance criterion requires every fracture end to remain unchanged. If the requirement means *pixel-identical source geometry*, reject all four as unproven against that stricter criterion; no fabricated zero-drift guarantee is supplied. No second attempt or unbounded retry was made.

Parent review disposition: after independently inspecting the complete four-source comparison and native32 strips, the parent accepted minor ice crack/bevel reinterpretation for these material sources, with the caveats above retained. All four are therefore proposed for selection under that explicitly stated tolerance, not under a pixel-identity standard.

## Isolated native32 verification

build_review.py reuses unchanged read_material/render_material helpers from the established exporter. It writes only this review directory and does not invoke the exporter CLI or select catalog entries. Sampling uses the existing Lanczos policy, original variant/NESW topology, historical alpha masks and opposite-edge matching. It produced four distinct blue-ice cells and sixteen distinct cells for each connected material.

Every alpha byte of each output strip equals the currently selected original runtime strip. Exact RGBA equality passed all 16 east-west and 16 south-north variant pairs, and all 64 east-west and 64 south-north allowed pairs for each NESW material. native-review.json contains detailed hashes and samples. These tests prove unchanged output silhouette and outermost-edge equality only, not pixel-identical interior motif alignment.

Visually inspected all-zoom2.png and every connected-grid-native32.png on black, white and olive backgrounds. Blue ice's broad shapes and saturated crevices remain readable; repeated tiles and differing quadrant motifs remain evident. Brittle ice remains a pale connected bar/capsule field due to the unchanged inherited masks, not an opaque ice floor. Deep factory remains dark with repeated central insets; the grille slats compress to a dark center at native size and black-background contrast remains low. Heated walkway keeps its bright cardinal heating strips; isolated zero-neighbor cells have little orange because the center stays unlit. None of those inherited limitations was masked or redesigned. Enlargement files are nearest-neighbor diagnostics, not HD assets.

Runtime integration, catalog selection, whole-pack tests and consumer review remain the parent's responsibility.
