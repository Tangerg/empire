## Secondary details and caveats

Small dust flecks change shape and position, but some linger. No claim is made that every fleck follows a continuous monotonic trajectory; the directional read comes primarily from the advancing ribbon cohorts and lower wisps. The final frame's lower-left wisp is renewal, not the old rightward wisp jumping back.

The two tufts are intended as stationary contacts. Their measured lower edges vary by at most 0.69 logical pixels on the left and 0.87 on the right. At the wrap they move upward by 0.65 and 0.87 logical pixels respectively. This is a disclosed residual source-drawing variation, not corrected with pixel shifting.

The total source alpha mass changes by under 1.8%, which argues against a large volume flash but is not motion evidence. Full and 3→4→1→2 seam sheets have been inspected at native 32/64 and zoom on black, white, bright stone, green ground, dark earth and checker. No backing card, clipping or visible exterior glow was found. Parent static-phase acceptance remains pending.
