- The five peripheral diamond identities persist, but their paths are independently authored and uneven. The upper-right fragment descends from the first to the aligning pose, while the others move less. No constant-speed particle interpolation is claimed.
- Main-assembly widths are 915, 905, 891, 791 source pixels. Selected closure-to-terminal contraction is 11.2% in width and 10.0% in height; the rejected first terminal contracted 23.6% in width. This is intentional restraint tightening, not whole-pad motion or per-frame fitting.

- Side-clasp diagnostic midlines span x623.25..627.5 and y627.25..641, within 0.095 and 0.307 logical pixels respectively. They support common visual registration but do not imply identical object silhouettes or a fixed physical emitter.
- The terminal double ring stays visible. There is no runtime loop, authored blank ending, extra final hold, scene removal time or 420/500 ms lifecycle. APNG encoding and static images are verified; browser and live-game playback remain unobserved.
