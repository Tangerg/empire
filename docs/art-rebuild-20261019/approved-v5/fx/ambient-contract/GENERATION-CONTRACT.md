Status: proposed source-derived generation contract, not artwork approval or deployment. No new art is generated by this audit. The candidate phase names in the accompanying patch must be agreed before registration; they are descriptive names for cyclic redraws, not labels already embedded in the original source.

## Authoritative mapping and preserved subjects

The complete, unmodified source is evidence/packages/story-candidate-01/assets/final-fantasy-v1/masters/fx/fx-atlas-03.png (1254×1254 RGB). Its 4×4 grid maps to four consecutive FX topics through build_final_fantasy_v1.py: atlas03 rows 1–4, columns 1–4. The topic catalog confirms the Chinese labels. Preserve these subjects, not an imagined weather symbol or weapon attack:

- C01-FX-09 秋雨 / autumn rain: blue-gray outlined falling drops, low pale-blue water beads and a shallow crown-shaped splash. Original columns show falling drops; drops plus small beads; drops plus crown; low beads alone
- C01-FX-10 河流浅水 / river shallows: two stationary gray-brown stones surrounded by thin, broken blue-gray elliptical ripples. Original columns show small, medium, broad, then smaller broken ripples. Keep stones; do not replace them with a blue disk or deep-water wave
- C01-FX-11 荒原强风 / strong wasteland wind: ochre-gold curved gust ribbons, small earthy flecks and wind-blown tufts/shards. Original columns show broad ribbon and tufts; curled ribbon and tufts; fragmented ribbon; sparse dust. The source supports a dry wind/debris effect, not lightning, frost, or a tornado
- C01-FX-12 山炉热浪 / mountain-forge heat: amber vertical wavering heat tongues above a stationary row of small dark coals/stones. Original columns show tall tongues; differently curled tall tongues; separated tongues; short tongues. Keep the coal bed and heat identity; do not substitute an explosion or tall ordinary-fire plume

Important source limitation: the rain, wind and heat rows taper toward remnants. Existing metadata says loop=true, but source identity alone does not prove a clean last-to-first seam. The redraw must resolve that conflict through authored renewal, retaining the subjects rather than reproducing the old pop. The source atlas is opaque RGB; its alpha intermediate still has white cell panels. Neither is a valid transparent master for the new exporter. Do not run legacy chroma/white flood removal or alpha edits on the new authored masters.

## Four authored cyclic phases

Each numbered phase is an independently authored drawing on the exact same canvas. The fourth phase is renewal, not a fade to an empty terminal frame.

### FX09
1. rainfall: falling drops at staggered heights, small continuing low water beads
2. splash-onset: leading drops descend; first low splashes form; a subsequent drop remains in the upper region
3. splash-crown: low crown broadens and breaks into beads; subsequent falling drops continue
4. rain-renewal: crown settles into beads as the next cohort occupies positions that continue naturally into phase 1

Track individual drop motion downward. Avoid a fresh full crown abruptly replacing flat beads on 4→1. Keep a stable contact level and consistent volume over the cycle; the next cohort must be authored, not generated by translating an entire previous frame.

### FX10
1. ripple-near: small broken elliptical rings around the same two stones
2. ripple-growing: the same outward-moving arcs expand while new inner arcs appear
3. ripple-wide: outer arcs are widest and fragment; inner renewal remains visible
4. ripple-renewal: outer arcs diminish while replacement near-stone arcs lead into phase 1

The stones and their contact points must not grow, drift, or bob. Do not reverse the outer rings inward to reset. Their disappearance and replacement must be authored as separate ripple cohorts.

### FX11
1. gust-curl-a: main ochre gust ribbon curves through the frame with a small moving debris cohort
2. gust-curl-b: curve advances/deforms; debris progresses in the same prevailing direction
3. gust-breakup: leading ribbon separates into smaller arcs; a following curl remains present
4. gust-renewal: leading debris exits or dissolves within the authored margins while the following curl leads into phase 1

Do not use the old dust-only final column as a hard reset. Avoid left/right oscillation, whole-image translation, a stationary ribbon plus opacity pulses, or a full wind wall suddenly appearing on 4→1.

### FX12
1. heat-curl-a: amber wavering columns rise from the fixed coal bed
2. heat-curl-b: column curves change and rise; lower portions renew at the same bed positions
3. heat-split: upper tongues separate and dissipate while lower replacement tongues remain
4. heat-renewal: the next lower tongues feed into phase 1 with a continuous upward rhythm

The coal bed stays fixed in shape, scale and location. Wavering heat should not jump sideways or grow from tiny embers to tall columns in one seam. All transparency and dissipation must be drawn into each generated image; no programmatic faded copies, alpha normalization, warped copies, optical flow or synthesized in-between frames.

## Fixed geometry and transparency

- Four 1254×1254 PNG RGBA masters per topic, each with genuine transparent exterior and a nonempty effect; preserve original generated files and hashes
- A single shared transform per topic, identical to the existing approved FX pipeline: scale 28/1254, logical origin [2,2], sourcePivot [627,627]
- This maps the whole source canvas to a 28×28 region of a 32×32 logical cell, with pivot [16,16]. At 2×, map to 56×56 inside 64×64 with origin [4,4] and pivot [32,32]
- Never alpha-bbox crop, per-phase fit, independently resize, independently shift, or re-anchor a phase. Measure stationary features in the masters; correct drawing inconsistencies through the image authoring tool
- Required output strips: 128×32 and 256×64; four cells left-to-right in contract order. Both use normal source-over alpha
- Source perimeter alpha may be at most 1 under the existing gate. Every final cell perimeter must be alpha0, and the intended 2px logical padding must remain clear. A source pass does not excuse visible clipped content, white cards, checkerboard pixels, magenta fringe, backing rectangles, or halos
- Translucent heat/water is valid and need not reach near-opaque alpha. An opaque coal/stone core is also valid. The exporter must preserve alpha rather than forcing every phase to a common maximum
- No damage text, white hit-circle, weather panel, ground disk, ground clipping mask, or baked scene background in the art

## Playback and seam review

Canonical timing is 12fps, four equal frames: start times 0, 83⅓, 166⅔, 250ms, then phase 1 at 333⅓ms. There is no final-frame hold and no 420ms removal rule for these catalog clips. Any future scene consumer must own creation/removal explicitly.

Visual approval must include all of the following, on each topic and both output densities:

1. View the entire 1→2→3→4→1 sequence at exact 12fps for at least eight uninterrupted cycles. Do not crossfade, interpolate, ping-pong, or hold the fourth frame
2. Inspect a labeled 3→4→1→2 seam strip alongside the full 1→2→3→4 strip. A slowed diagnostic replay is useful in addition to the required exact-speed replay
3. Track the actual falling drops, outward ripple arcs, directional gust fragments, and rising heat tongues through 4→1. Reject teleportation, obvious motion reversal, a large density flash, sudden silhouette replacement, or scale/pivot jump
4. Check fixed landmarks: FX10's two stones and FX12's coal bed must remain registered. FX09's splash-contact height stays fixed. FX11's moving debris must follow its own motion rather than move because the whole canvas shifts
5. Composite on black, white, bright stone, green ground, dark earth, and a diagnostic checker. Inspect both native 32px and 64px, plus a large zoom for edge defects. Do not mistake a drawn checker or white interior card for alpha
6. Keep raw master hashes, rendered frame hashes, placement, alpha statistics, exact timing, sampled seam frames and reviewer findings. Unique hashes and alpha thresholds are mechanical checks, never evidence of meaningful motion or a good seam
7. Distinguish a staged renderer demo from actual deployment. Currently no authored campaign scene/weapon/heal binding references FX09–12. Renderer playback evidence cannot be labeled live gameplay or a newly deployed environmental effect
