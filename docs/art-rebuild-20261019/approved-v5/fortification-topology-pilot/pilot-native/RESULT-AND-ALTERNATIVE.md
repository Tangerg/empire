# Bounded wall module pilot result

No usable registered wall source was accepted. Mask5 received one initial generation and exactly one parent-approved native edit. Masks10/7/13 were not generated. All original raw outputs and exact prompts are preserved. No source pixel cleanup, fitting, shifting, stretching or painting was performed, and nothing entered production.

Initial: required cap strong-alpha coverage51.4%; N/S sampled registered cuts0%. Edit: cap99.5%, E-face81.5%, joined cuts99.8%/99.5%. These alpha figures do not establish material continuity: the edited top/right-face break moved roughly40px east from its required x783.75. The expected lower-right face region is partly absent and partly top-facing material. Clipping can remove excess material but cannot create the missing face or relocate its shading. The edit also leaves a small cap deficit at the left boundary. Exact geometry therefore remains a failure.

## Proposed lower-risk material-source approach, not implemented

Separate the generative art problem from the exact polygon problem:

1. Generate a large, broad, nearly uniform stone top-material sheet with generous opaque stock and sparse coarse slab structure. Do not ask it to depict the registered solid or its exterior faces. This is painted material input, not a replacement96px sprite.
2. Generate independent south/front and east/side masonry face stocks with coherent mortar rhythm and fixed upper-left lighting. Give them generous material around their intended cuts; do not hide terminal shadows in the stock.
3. If separately approved, let one renderer-owned geometric contract supply the exact full32x32 cap polygons and exposed face polygons. Use one common fixed material scale/projection rather than arbitrary per-variant scaling. Source texture is clipped or UV-mapped under that shared contract; no code-painted stone texture, no rotations of already-lit solids, and no fitting variants independently.
4. Prove actual source opacity, bond/lighting continuity, passage clearance and all16 topology states before production. A generated material sheet is not automatically tileable. Exposed cap borders, corners, concave contacts and repeated texture seams still need explicit fixtures and review.

This is a proposal requiring parent review of the material-to-geometry contract. It is not an implementation claim or permission to stretch the rejected module into place. Existing mission rules, wall cells, actors, gates and old registered props remain unchanged.
